A Steady Start

Steady & Strong

Your 4-day plan. Machines with back support, no squats, no deadlifts, about an hour a day.

Before you start, every day

  • Breathe out when you push or pull. Never hold your breath.
  • Move slow and controlled. No rushing, no jerking the weight.
  • Stop and rest if you feel sharp pain, numbness, or dizziness. Feeling tired is normal — pain is not.
When in doubt, stop. A little stiffness is okay. Sharp back or leg pain is your sign to rest and check in with your doctor.
