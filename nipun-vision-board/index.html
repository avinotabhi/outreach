Four directions, one you

Built off what you told us on the call: black, teal, and royal blue, a saturated look, fast cuts in the style of Parikshit's B-roll instinct, and the energy TechBurner brings to his edits. Each board below takes that same raw material somewhere slightly different. Pick the one whose sample line feels most like something you'd actually say on camera, that's the direction we build from.

Option A Night Mode

Black-dominant with a single teal signal. Reads as precise and a little technical, closest to the captioning-tool DNA of the brand. This is the register for when you're explaining something and want to look sharp doing it.
