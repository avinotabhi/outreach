SCALE & USAGE

Keep the hierarchy consistent across platforms so the account is recognizable before anyone reads a word.

05 / LINKEDIN & X TONALITY

Same voice, different throttle

Blunt and technical everywhere, the platform decides how fast and how formal

The voice doesn't change between platforms, LinkedIn and X just turn different dials, formality and speed, on the same underlying bluntness. Nothing here should read like it was written by two different people.

LinkedIn

Blunt with structure, built for people evaluating you, not just entertained by you

  • Open with the claim or the number, never a warm-up sentence
  • Short paragraphs, line breaks over dense blocks, this gets read at a desk, not scrolled past
  • Story and technical depth both earn their length here, a longer post is fine when it's doing real work
  • No hashtags, no "Thoughts?" or "Agree?" as a closer, the point should generate the comment on its own
  • Founder Diaries and Build Log carry more weight here than pure hot takes

X

The rawest version of you, closer to how you'd text a friend about the industry

  • Fragments are fine, full sentences are optional
  • The contrarian edge goes furthest here, LinkedIn softens it slightly, X doesn't have to
  • Threads carry the Build Log pillar, one beat per tweet, no throat-clearing
  • React to what's happening in the industry in near real time, the one place pillar-agnostic posts are fine
  • Humor and memes are fair game here in a way they aren't on LinkedIn

The Manifesto and The Person pillars travel well on both platforms, adjust the delivery, not the substance.

07 / MOOD & IMAGERY

Unpolished over produced

The build should look like a build, not a stock photo

NATURAL LIGHT, DESK SETUPS
TEXTURE, PAPER, WOOD, LINEN
LATE NIGHT SCREENS, REAL WORK
ONE BOLD ACCENT, USED ONCE

LEAN INTO

  • Real desks, real prototypes, real whiteboards, not staged shots
  • Natural light over studio lighting
  • Screen recordings and raw build footage
  • Team faces, not just your own

AVOID

  • Stock photo gloss or generic "startup" imagery
  • Cold blues, greys, anything that reads corporate SaaS
  • Logo animations or branded intros at the start of a video
  • Forced positivity, marketing speak, exclamation points doing the work

SHOT NOTES BY PILLAR

  • The Manifesto: straight to camera, tight frame, nothing distracting behind you
  • Build Log: screen recordings plus a wide desk shot, hands actually on the keyboard
  • Founder Diaries: natural light, a slightly looser frame, let the pauses stay in
  • Team & Culture: candid, not posed, catch people mid-conversation
  • The Person: wherever you actually are, the desk, the drive, the walk to get coffee
08 / SOUND & MUSIC

Techno or rock, nothing softer

Your own call, direct: techno or rock for edits and videos, matched to the pillar doing the talking

Techno

Motion, momentum, the build actually happening

  • Build Log cuts, screen recordings, fast product montages
  • The Person pillar, quick personality hits
  • Instrumental only under voiceover, so the track drives without fighting your words

Rock

Edge, defiance, the contrarian and the vulnerable

  • The Manifesto pillar, anything with a "phones are broken" energy
  • Founder Diaries, a stripped-down or slower rock cut for the heavier beats
  • Full-vocal drops reserved for cut-only montages with no voiceover competing

Avoid generic corporate lo-fi, acoustic coffee-shop tracks, or anything that reads as stock "epic corporate" music, none of that matches your voice.
