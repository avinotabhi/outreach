01 · The Vision

The funnel stops in one place. This is what comes after.

Right now, a webinar sells a ₹4,000–6,000 course, and everyone who doesn't buy on that call simply disappears. The Speaking Community is the missing layer — a low-priced, high-engagement monthly membership built on one idea: people don't get fluent by watching more videos. They get fluent by speaking out loud, getting corrected, and doing it again the next day.

Not a replacement

This isn't competing with the high-ticket courses — it's a feeder. It re-engages the audience that already said "not yet" at ₹4,000+, and gives them a lower-risk way to start.

A proof engine

Members who get real results in the community become your best upsell candidates for the premium courses — motivated, proven, and already inside your world.

04 · Core Features

Built as a daily practice habit, not a content library.

Every feature exists to get members speaking, getting feedback, and coming back tomorrow.

Buddy System

Every new member is paired with an accountability partner on day one — nobody shows up to an empty room.

Daily Speaking Feed

A topic is posted daily; members record a 60–90 second response and get structured peer feedback.

Trainer's Pick

One submitted video is highlighted daily — every member is implicitly competing to be featured.

Streaks & Levels

Consecutive-day streaks plus a 4-stage progression so members always know where they stand.

Word of the Day

A daily vocabulary drop, tied to the feed — use today's word in tomorrow's spoken video.

Weekly Live Session

A hired trainer runs roleplay simulations — interviews, calls, presentations — not lectures.

Doubt Resolution

An always-on thread for quick grammar and vocabulary questions, answered fast.

Certificates

Brandable milestone certificates designed to be posted on Instagram and LinkedIn — free marketing.

Referral Rewards

One free month for every friend referred who stays active 30+ days.

07 · Go-to-Market

Three phases, in order.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4

Activate the warm list

The 25,000 webinar registrants are the fastest, cheapest path to the first paying cohort. Runs as a Founding Member launch: share the idea → cast the vision → invitation (100 spots at ₹699) → one clear CTA → 7–10 day follow-up sequence.

Phase 2 · Weeks 5–12

Organic layer

Founding member wins — speaking transformations, certificates, streaks — become YouTube/Instagram content, with a soft CTA at the end of relevant videos. Kept separate from the paid course funnel to avoid channel conflict.

Phase 3 · Ongoing

Closed-cohort scaling

Enrollment reopens at ₹999/month on a quarterly cycle rather than staying open indefinitely — preserves scarcity, keeps cohorts cohesive, and gives a natural rhythm for fresh promotional pushes.
