01 / THE WEEKLY RHYTHM

Five weekdays, five pillars

Weekends off on purpose, this runs on your actual bandwidth, not an influencer's. Each day carries one pillar so nothing competes with itself, and the topics underneath rotate between technology, the build, and the self-growth side of running this company.
