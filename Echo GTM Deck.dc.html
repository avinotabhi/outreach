Two Different Jobs

Before launch and after launch need two different plans.

Before we launch, the only job is getting as many real, interested people as possible onto the waitlist. After we launch, the job changes completely — it's about making sure the people who join actually keep paying every month, not just for the first one.

The Price We'll Offer

A real early-bird price, not just a marketing line.

Only 500 people get ₹499. Once those spots are gone, the price moves to ₹549, then to ₹599. This has to happen automatically the moment each batch sells out — that's a feature we need to ask the tech lead for directly.

WhatsApp Messages

Four messages sent to the 20,000–30,000 people who already showed interest before: the announcement, a feature reveal, proof it works, then a 48-hour countdown.

Anyone who replies or clicks gets a call within the hour. This is the single most effective way to actually close a sale — not just a nice-to-have.

03

A short series to the existing list — Janhavi's own story of why she built this, then a feature walkthrough, then testimonials, then urgency near launch.

04

YouTube + Instagram Posts

Three stages — first just curiosity with no reveal, then the full feature video with pricing announced, then countdown posts as spots run out.

Part 2 — After Launch

Getting members is only half the job. Keeping them is the other half.

Every number on this page assumes people keep paying after month one. Here's how we make sure that actually happens.

Keeping Members

Reminders before every renewal

A message 2–3 days before each payment is due, gentle nudges to keep people using the app, and an automatic message trying to win back anyone who cancels.

Community

Echo's own Instagram page grows up

Starts by sharing posts with Janhavi's main page, then grows into its own thing — real members, their stories, milestones — once there's an actual community to show.

Growth

Referrals keep working after launch

What starts as a launch offer — bring a friend, get a free month — just keeps running afterward, using the same tracking links we set up from day one.

Part 2 — After Launch
Tracking

We watch what's actually working

How many people join, how many leave, and how much each member is worth — broken down by which post, message, or channel brought them in. This tells us where to spend more, and where to stop.

Trial Option

A ₹29 day pass for people who just want to try it

Full access for 24 hours, one-time only per phone number so it can't be reused. For people who liked what they saw but weren't ready to commit to a month — if they like the day, we prompt them straight into the ₹499/₹549/₹599 plan.

Content Funnel

Free PDFs move into the app

The free guides Janhavi already gives away in her videos — pronunciation, common mistakes, interview English — now live inside Echo instead of being a plain download. Every video becomes a reason to install the app, not just give an email.
