A Content Partnership for Idanta AI

Prepared by J Curve by Avi  ·  September 2026

The opportunity in front of you

Idanta AI is building something genuinely rare: a patented identity layer that most of the market doesn't know exists yet. That is both the advantage and the challenge.

The brands you are selling to, and the packaging partners who will bundle your technology into their own offering, need to understand what you do before they can trust it enough to buy it. Right now you are pre-seed, pre-launch in the public sense, and already fielding inbound interest on the strength of the technology alone. The next few months are about turning that early credibility into a visible, consistent presence, without overspending before the raise closes.

What we're proposing

Platforms

LinkedIn (primary) + X

One set of content, posted natively to both. LinkedIn is where your actual buyers, brand and packaging decision-makers, spend their time. X builds parallel visibility for the founders and the technology story, without doubling production cost.

Weekly cadence

4 pieces a week, across both platforms

2 videos and 2 photo or graphic posts (with captions) each week. Scripted, edited, and posted by us, end to end.

Monthly flagship

One flagship video every month

A more polished, story-driven piece, built around whatever is most worth telling that month, a pilot milestone, an industry perspective, a team story. Scoped to a single shoot session and one round of revisions, so it stays sustainable at a monthly pace.

Strategy

4 strategy calls a month

Weekly check-ins so the content stays fed by what's actually happening in your sales pipeline, and your sales conversations stay informed by what's resonating in the market.

Included

Scripting, editing, publishing, amplification coordination

We handle the writing, the edit, and the posting. We also coordinate reshares and comments from your founders and team on the posts that matter most.

What we'll need from you

  • A short weekly voice note on what's moving, a pilot update, a conversation worth sharing, so we can plan content ahead of time instead of chasing it.
  • Timely feedback on drafts, so the weekly cadence doesn't stall waiting on approvals.
  • Your presence on the 4 monthly strategy calls.
  • Founders and team reshared or commenting when we flag a pillar post for amplification.

Room to grow

This is intentionally the lighter version of what this partnership can become. Once your seed round closes, or at a fixed 60-day check-in at the latest, we'll revisit scope together, most likely adding dedicated X growth, event and webinar production support, and a heavier cadence, priced accordingly. Starting light now protects your runway. Growing later should reflect the size of the story you'll actually be telling by then.
