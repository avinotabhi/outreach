Direction locked: High Voltage

Four shades of loud

He picked High Voltage, Anton headlines, DM Sans body, black stage, royal blue signal. These four keep that exact pattern and only move the accent, so the choice left is which specific blue (and whether teal joins it) actually feels right once it's on screen.
